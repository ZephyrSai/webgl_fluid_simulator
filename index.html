Simulation

Touch, pen & gestures
  • Drag with any number of fingers to paint. Left→right plays low→high notes, higher on screen sounds brighter, faster strokes play denser runs.
  • Tap drops ink and rings a bell. Three-finger tap fires a color burst.
  • Hold still to sustain a note; the fluid pulses with the beat under your finger.
  • Pinch / spread two or more fingers to implode / explode the fluid and sweep the filter.
  • Twist two or more fingers to spin a vortex and play a scale run.
  • Apple Pencil / stylus: pressure sets stroke size and loudness, tilt widens the stroke and adds breath.
  • The music builds from ambient to intense the more you play, and settles when you stop.
  • iPad tip: iPadOS reserves four- and five-finger swipes and pinches for itself, and no web page can override that. To play with all ten fingers, turn those gestures off in Settings › Multitasking & Gestures. Adding Fluid Studio to your Home Screen also hides Safari's toolbars and edge-swipe navigation.
